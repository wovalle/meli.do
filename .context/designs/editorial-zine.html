A salami that
crossed an ocean.

Estelar | Made by… Tú Sabe is the first artisanal Dominican salami to reach New York. A product that travels with a flavor, a history, and an identity rooted in home. Our task was to build a brand worthy of that crossing.

Every detail — from the warm color palette to the artisanal typography — evokes authenticity, tradition, and a bit of Dominican sabor. The system flexes from the shelf to the street: packaging, signage, campaign.

"A proud moment for every Dominican represented on an American shelf."

The project launched in Santo Domingo and New York simultaneously. Social campaign, vendor collateral, and retail-shelf photography followed. On-shelf presence tested against American category leaders — colors held, lockup read clean, category signals felt Dominican first.

024 Feature / 02 Mellen — Issue 01

Thirty years,
one confident
mark.

02
ClientAlkasa · Year2024 · RoleArt Director

Alkasa has been building the Dominican Republic for thirty years. The brief: an anniversary identity that nods to the brand's past without getting stuck in it — a mark and a graphic system that could carry signage, print, and social, all while feeling like Alkasa.

The solution: a flexible "30" monogram, a deep brand-red anchored with cream, and a hand-drawn texture library that references construction sites, site plans, and hand-signed documents. A system built to adapt, not to impose.

"A graphic language that honors three decades of building the country."

036 Essay / 03 Mellen — Issue 01

On sabor & systems.

Portrait, Santo Domingo · 2024
Melissa Encarnacion
Head of Design, Liquid Digital

Ten years in, the thing I keep returning to is warmth. A brand can be rigorous and systemic and still feel like a kitchen — like somebody put the coffee on. That's what I'm after.

At Liquid I lead a team of five designers. We work for clients across the Dominican Republic and in the US — financial, insurance, FMCG, cultural, small-and-mighty founders shipping their first product.

The work I'm proudest of usually has an ingredient you can't fake: it's specific. To a place, a moment, a voice. Estelar is Dominican and proud. Alkasa is thirty and it looks thirty. A brand that could be from anywhere is usually from nowhere.

I studied design in Santo Domingo, spent time observing New York, and chose to build my career back home. The Caribbean is a generous teacher. Color works harder here. Type has to breathe.
