Dear reader,

I grew up in the Dominican Republic and spent a decade figuring out what it meant to design from here. I lead a team of five at Liquid Digital Agency in Santo Domingo. We've shipped identities for thirteen brands across food, finance, insurance, and culture.

My taste tips towards warm. I care about typography, paper weight, the temperature of a color. I like brands that feel like someone's actually at home behind the logo.

I studied design here, traveled enough to know the difference, and chose to stay. The Caribbean makes you brave with color. I try to earn that.

con cariño,
