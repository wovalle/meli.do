Design

Ten years ago I picked up design as a way to tell stories. Today I lead a team of five at Liquid Digital Agency in Santo Domingo, shaping identities for brands across the Dominican Republic and the diaspora.

My work sits between editorial craft and commercial rigor. I care about typography, color temperature, and the small moments of a brand that make people feel something real.
