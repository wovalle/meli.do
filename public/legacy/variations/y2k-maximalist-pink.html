TEN YEARS.
ONE LOUD,
ONE QUIET.

I'm a Dominican Art Director. For the past decade I've been shaping brand systems out of Santo Domingo — five of them at Liquid Digital Agency, where I now lead a team of five designers.

My work lives between two speeds: the loud surface where brands compete for attention, and the quiet craft that makes them worth remembering. I try to do both well, at once.
